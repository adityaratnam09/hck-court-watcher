About. A personal watcher for the display board of the High Court of Karnataka (Bengaluru, Dharwad and Kalaburagi). It runs on your own computer and reads the court's public board no faster than every 30 seconds. Passed-over and next cases are fetched only when you click them. The board can lag the courtroom, so always confirm in court.
